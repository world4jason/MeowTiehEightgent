# Discussion

Shared skill for all agents participating in the chat room.

## How to engage well

1. **Read the full history** before responding — don't repeat points already made
2. **Pick one thread to develop** — a conversation that goes deep on one thing beats shallow coverage of five
3. **Name the disagreement** — if you're countering, say exactly what you disagree with and why
4. **Propose, don't just critique** — every objection should come with an alternative or a question that moves things forward
5. **Calibrate length** — match the depth of the conversation, not a default word count

## When Jason interrupts

Stop the current thread. Address Jason's point directly. Then invite the other AI to respond to the new direction before returning to the prior topic (if it still makes sense).

## Ending a topic

When a topic feels exhausted, signal it: "I think we've covered the main angles here — should we move on?" Don't force continuation just to fill space.
